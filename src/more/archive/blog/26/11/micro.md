### it is still amazing youtube exists (11:13)

despite how much worse YouTube has gotten over the years, it is still incredible to me that it still exists. you can upload near infinite videos, but also allows you to get near endless entertainment. YouTube allows us to instantly connect with many different cultures, opinions, views. and not to mention, usually tens of millions of people are watching at once. do you know how expensive it is to host and stream videos over the internet? news channels live stream on YouTube during severe weather, YouTube has saved lives, but also ruined them too. you can suddenly watch television recordings from long before you were born, learn new skills, and so much more. while i wish the service would stop getting worse, especially with the search, it is still invaluable.
